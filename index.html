How it works

Everyone is a plate around the table

Each person gets a coloured, numbered plate. Plate 1 is you. Put each item on the plates that had it, and the totals work themselves out.

  1. 1

    Enter the bill

    Choose how many people are at the table, then type the subtotal and the tax & fees from the receipt.

  2. 2

    Assign items

    Type an item’s price and drag it to a plate. Or tap the centre, then every plate that shared it. Shared items split equally.

  3. 3

    Pick a tip

    Each total includes its share of tax and tip. Change the tip and every total updates, and still adds up.

  4. 4

    Share and finish

    Tap a plate to see its tally and send it as text. Finish saves the bill to your history.

Just splitting evenly? After you enter the bill, FairBite offers Split bill equally with each person’s amount.

Why FairBite

A split that explains itself

A bare number starts the “hang on, that doesn’t add up” conversation. FairBite shows what every total is made of.

Fair to the cent

Tax, fees and tip are shared in proportion to what each person ordered. Every plate adds up, and all the plates add up to the bill, exactly.

The reason, not just the number

Every total breaks down into items, fees and tip, so anyone can check it at the table.

No account, no ads, no tracking

Nothing to sign up for. No ads, no analytics. Your bills stay on your phone.

Works offline

Splitting a bill never needs the internet, so bad restaurant Wi-Fi doesn’t matter. Only buying or restoring the unlock goes online.

No dragging required

Every action works with taps alone, and every control is labelled for TalkBack. Plates show their number, not just their colour.

History when you need it

Finished bills are saved in Bill history, with every plate’s breakdown. Name them, rename them or delete them.
