FairBite Privacy Policy

Effective: October 23, 2026

FairBite is a bill-splitting app for Android, made by ccspart2 (Charlie Castro), an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what FairBite does with information. The short version: FairBite has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your bills stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your bills. Amounts, items, plates, tips and bill history are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere, and they are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. If you uninstall FairBite or clear its data, they are gone.
  • Your settings. Your default tip and default party size are stored on your device the same way.

When information leaves your phone

FairBite uses the internet only for purchases. Everything else works offline.

  1. Sharing a tally. If you tap Share, FairBite hands the summary text to the app you choose (for example a messaging app). That only happens when you start it. What the other app does with it is governed by its own policy.
  2. Buying or restoring the unlock. FairBite offers one free split, then a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited splits.
    • Google Play processes the payment. I never see your card or payment details, or your name or email.
    • RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, checks and records the purchase on my behalf. It receives:
      • the purchase information (product, price, currency, date and Google Play's purchase token);
      • a random anonymous ID that FairBite creates for the purchase;
      • basic technical details needed to process it, such as the app version and the device's language and country.
    • This is used only to confirm and restore your purchase. It is never used for advertising and never sold. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  3. Google Block Store. On builds from Google Play, FairBite saves a small "free split used" marker so the free split isn't granted again after reinstalling.
    • The RevenueCat software may also save its anonymous purchase ID there.
    • Block Store is part of your Google account's backup on your device. It is end-to-end encrypted when your device supports it, and I cannot read it.
    • It contains no bill data.

What FairBite does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files access.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

FairBite is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your bills: use Settings → Clear bill history, or uninstall the app.
  • Purchase records: you can ask me to delete the purchase record RevenueCat holds for you. Email me and, if you can, include the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) from your purchase receipt so I can find it.
    • Google Play keeps its own record of your order under Google's policies.
    • Deleting the RevenueCat record does not refund or cancel the purchase. Restoring later may re-create it.
  • You can also ask what information about your purchase I have access to.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
