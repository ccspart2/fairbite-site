I got a new phone. How do I get my purchase back?

Your purchase belongs to the Google account you bought it with. On the new phone, sign in to Google Play with that same account, install FairBite, and then either:

  • open Settings → Unlimited splits → Restore purchase, or
  • tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen that appears when you start a new split.

Restoring needs an internet connection. If it says no purchase was found, check that Google Play is signed in to the account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Within 48 hours of buying, you can request a refund directly from Google Play: open your Google Play order history, find FairBite, and choose to request a refund. After 48 hours, email me at ccspart2@gmail.com with your GPA. order number and I’ll help.

It says “Google Play can’t confirm this purchase”

This means Google Play reports the purchase on your account, but FairBite couldn’t confirm it. Email me at ccspart2@gmail.com with the Google Play order number from your receipt (it starts with GPA.), and I’ll sort it out.

It says “Payment pending”

Some payment methods take a while to clear. FairBite unlocks by itself once Google Play confirms the payment. If it stays pending for a long time, check the order in Google Play.

What counts as my free split?

Your first bill counts once it reaches the Summary screen, where everyone’s totals appear. Starting a bill and leaving it earlier doesn’t use it up. The How it works demo never counts. After the free split, the unlock screen appears when you start your next bill, before you’ve typed anything, never halfway through one. A bill already in progress, Bill history and Settings are always available.

Does FairBite work offline?

Yes. Splitting, sharing a tally, Bill history and Settings all work with no connection. Only buying or restoring the unlock needs the internet. If you’ve already unlocked, being offline never blocks you.

Which devices does FairBite support?

  • Android 8.0 or later.
  • Phones, in landscape. FairBite is designed for a phone held on its side, so the whole table fits on screen. If your phone doesn’t turn, switch on Auto-rotate.
  • Tablets and foldables are supported: tablets in landscape, and foldables opened flat. On a tablet held upright FairBite asks you to turn it sideways; a half-folded foldable is asked to open flat.
  • Split screen and pop-up windows aren’t supported. FairBite shows a message asking for the full screen; nothing you’ve entered is lost.
  • On a very small screen, or with a very large display size, FairBite may say it doesn’t fit. A smaller display size in your device’s settings fixes it.

How is the stray cent split fairly?

Money doesn’t always divide evenly. $10.00 shared by three people is $3.333… each, and somebody has to pay $3.34. FairBite handles that the same careful way every time:

  • Shared items split equally between the plates that had them.
  • Tax & fees and the tip are shared in proportion to what each plate ordered: order more, pay more of the tax and tip.
  • Each part is rounded on its own (each item, the fees, the tip) so every line on a tally adds up to the total under it, and the plates add up to the whole bill to the cent.
  • Leftover cents go first to whoever was closest to the next cent. If that’s a dead heat, the cent goes to whoever has had the fewest extra cents so far on this bill, so they spread around the table. If it’s still a tie, it goes to you, plate 1, because you’re the one holding the phone.

Nothing is random: the same bill always gives the same numbers, so changing the tip never shuffles anyone’s items.

Can I use FairBite without dragging, or with TalkBack?

Yes. Dragging is only a shortcut: every item can be assigned by tapping the centre and then the plates that had it, which is also how shared items work. Every control has a TalkBack label, and plates always show their number as well as their colour.
